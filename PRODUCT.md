# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary users are interviewers and hiring managers scanning Cookies' portfolio for comprehensive internship roles across AI product, content operations, growth, and education-oriented product work. They usually decide quickly whether the candidate has a clear position, credible ownership, and measurable impact.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio helps an interviewer understand in 10 seconds who Cookies is: an AI scenario application and implementation practitioner with 0-to-1 product design and full-cycle operations ability. In 3 minutes, it should communicate the strongest proof points: AI comic generation, automated ad creative production, MeowMind, education公益 content, and growth operations.

Success means the visitor can quickly answer:
- What role Cookies fits.
- Which three projects are strongest.
- What methods Cookies owns from discovery to delivery.
- What measurable outcomes prove impact.
- How to contact or download the resume.

## Brand Personality

Creative, energetic, and accountable. The experience should feel like a living case board from someone who can turn fuzzy AI ideas into shipped workflows, measurable efficiency gains, and human-centered products. The tone is warm and confident, not corporate.

## Anti-references

Avoid ordinary resume templates, Word-like profile pages, cold SaaS blue-purple gradients, generic metric-card hero sections, and pages that are visually busy but unclear about responsibilities or outcomes.

## Design Principles

- Ten-second clarity: the first screen must state identity, role fit, proof, and next action without requiring scrolling.
- Three-minute narrative: projects unfold as a guided path from problem to role to method to result.
- Evidence over adjectives: every major claim should connect to a concrete output, metric, tool, or artifact.
- Creative but hireable: visual personality should make Cookies memorable while keeping reading speed and credibility high.
- Easy to update: portfolio content lives in structured JSON and Markdown so projects, skills, and contact details can change without rewriting UI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use strong contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, semantic HTML, responsive layouts, and visible focus states. Motion can be expressive, but it must respect reduced-motion preferences and never block reading or navigation.
